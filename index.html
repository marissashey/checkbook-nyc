About

This visualization explores New York City's annual spending using data from Checkbook NYC, revealing spending surpassing $140 billion in FY 2025—larger than most U.S. states and significantly outpacing other major American cities.

FY 2025 Major City Spending Comparison

New York City $140+ billion1
Washington, DC $21.2 billion2
Chicago $16–17 billion3
San Francisco $15.9 billion4
Los Angeles $13.95 billion5
Philadelphia $11.83 billion6
New York City's spending exceeds the combined spending of the other five cities.

Three departments dominate NYC's spending landscape:

1. Department of Education $38B
2. Department of Social Services $15B
3. New York Police Department $11B

Despite these large allocations, the city has a wide range of agencies. 89 agencies operated with less than $10 million each, demonstrating the breadth of municipal coverage.

Data Currency: Figures current as of September 2, 2025. NYC's real-time financial transparency through Checkbook NYC provides more current data than many other municipalities still finalizing their FY 2025 reports.
Methodology + Notes
• For visual clarity, segments below display thresholds consolidated in "Other"
• "OTPS" = Other Than Personal Services; "PS" = Personal Services
• Minor aggregation variations may occur due to visualization processing
• Data sourced from Checkbook NYC API and processed for interactive display
